# About Us

## Engineering-First Smart Home Hardware

Apex Sensor Labs LLC is an engineering-first company dedicated to designing and building high-performance, battery-efficient smart home devices. We focus on open, local protocols such as Zigbee and ESP-NOW, ensuring your home automation setup is future-proof and entirely local.

### One Engineer, Endless Innovation

Apex Sensor Labs is a single-engineer operation. From initial PCB design and case modeling to firmware development and website management, every aspect of our products is crafted by a single hand. This allows for a level of architectural consistency and vertical integration that is rarely found in mass-market consumer electronics.

!!! info "The Philosophy"
    "I build the hardware I actually want to use in my own home. That means no cloud dependencies, extreme battery life, and a focus on local-first control."

### Our Process

Every Apex product follows a rigorous development lifecycle:

1.  **Custom PCB Design:** We design our own ESP32-based boards, specifically optimized for ultra-low power consumption.
2.  **Bespoke Case Design:** Enclosures are designed in-house to be compact, functional, and aesthetic.
3.  **Low-Level Firmware:** Our firmware is written to maximize efficiency and minimize setup friction for the end-user.
4.  **Community Integration:** We build with the broader open smart home ecosystem in mind, ensuring compatibility with platforms like Home Assistant.

### The Mission

Apex Sensor Labs aims to solve the "hidden" problems of smart homes: the need for frequent battery changes, the unreliability of cloud-based bridges, and the lack of accurate presence detection. By focusing on specialized hardware like **Apex Core** and **Crosswalk**, we're bringing professional-grade, reliable, and privacy-respecting automation to every home.

**Our Philosophy: Your Home, Your Setup.**
As a hardware company, we want to bring our high-performance devices into your home, regardless of your existing ecosystem. We plan to offer multiple firmware versions for our hardware, allowing you to choose the protocol (Zigbee, ESP-NOW, etc.) that best fits your environment. We build the hardware; you decide how it communicates.
