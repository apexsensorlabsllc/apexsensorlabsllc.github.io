# Getting Started with Apex Core

The **Apex Core** is not just another Zigbee bridge; it's a dynamic gateway designed to bring the longevity and reliability of Zigbee into your smart home. 

Unlike traditional bridges that hard-code device types, Apex Core is designed to be **protocol-agnostic** and **future-proof**.

Follow these instructions to set up your new Apex Core.

---

## Unboxing

Your Apex Core package includes everything you need to start bridging your home.

![[Core]](./pictures/ApexCore.jpeg)

*   **1x Apex Core Bridge:** The heart of your Zigbee network.
*   **1x Apex Core Case:** A 3D printed case that houses the Apex Core
*   **1x 2.4GHz Antenna:** Antenna for Wi-Fi connections
*   **1x SMA to IPEX/u.FL connector:** Connector for the ESP32 antenna
*   **1x Quick Start Card:** Featuring your unique support links.

---

## Hardware Overview

The Apex Core is built on a custom-designed PCB engineered for maximum reliability and local performance.

*   **Processor:** High-performance **ESP32-C6** SoC.
*   **Radios:** Integrated **Zigbee 3.0** and smart home bridging support.
*   **Power:** USB-C (5V) powered with ultra-low standby current.
*   **Feedback:** Multi-color RGB LED for instant status diagnostics.
*   **Control:** Physical **BOOT button** for manual pairing and reset management.
