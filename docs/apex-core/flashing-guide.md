# Step 1: Flashing Firmware

The first step is to put the latest "brain" (firmware) onto your Apex Core. You do this right in your browser.

!!! tip "Why we Flash First"
    To ensure you have the latest features and the most secure, privacy-focused version of our local software, we start every setup by quickly updating the device's firmware. This ensures your hardware is running the best possible version of Apex Core before it connects to your smart home.

!!! info "Browser Requirement"
    You **must** use **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** on a computer to flash your device. We do not support other browsers.

---

### Preparation
- **Apex Core**
- **USB-C Cable** (the one included with your phone or the bridge)
- **Computer** with Chrome or Edge installed.

### Flashing Procedure

![[Pairing Mode]](./pictures/1-Core_Red.jpeg)

These instructions are examples of what the following Web Flasher will look like. Please review them and then proceed to the next step.

1. **Hold Boot:** Hold the BOOT button of your Apex Core. This is the large button visible with the case lid on.

2. **Connect:** Plug your Apex Core into your computer's USB port while the BOOT button is still held. Once connected, release the BOOT button.

3. **Generate Credentials:** Click **Generate Credentials** to create your on-the-fly QR code and pin.

  ![[QR_Code]](./pictures/2-Generate_Creds.jpg)

  **Save your generated code as a screenshot you will need it later.**

  ![[QR_Code]](./pictures/3-Creds_Generated.jpg)

4.  **Connect in Browser:** Click the **Flash Device** button on the web page and select your USB COM port, and click **Connect**.

  ![[Connect]](./pictures/4-Flash_Device.jpg)

  ![[Connect_Port]](./pictures/5-USB_Flash.jpg)

5. **Install Firmware:** Click Install Apex Core

  ![[Install]](./pictures/6-Install.jpg)

6. **Erase Device:** Click the Erase device checkbox, then click Next.

  ![[Erase]](./pictures/7-Erase_Data.jpg)

7. **Confirm:** Click Install. Wait for it to finish and then click done. Once finished, unplug your device and place it in your desired location.

  ![[Confirm]](./pictures/8-Install_final.jpg)
